Terms of service for Moneypenny Artifact Pilot

This personal integration is for the operator and accounts the operator expressly authorizes. Its purpose is to publish Moneypenny’s generated run log, audit report and dashboard to the operator’s configured Google Drive folder.

The operator controls the destination and the cloud routines that can call the service. Use the integration only with an account and data you are authorized to process. Review the requested Drive permissions when connecting an account. If delivery is uncertain, retain the operation ID and check its recorded state before attempting recovery.

You can discontinue access by revoking the app in your Google Account. For help with stored artifacts or operation records, use the support address displayed on the app’s Google consent screen.
